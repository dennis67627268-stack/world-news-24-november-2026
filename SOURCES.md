## Gaza strike

Israeli strikes on Tuesday killed Naeem Abu Nahel, also reported as Naim Abu Nahl, head of Hamas’s internal security service, in western Gaza City. Health officials and first responders said at least six other Palestinians were killed in earlier strikes that day, including at a police post in al-Mawasi, Khan Younis, and in Wadi Gaza. Israel said it struck a senior Hamas figure and “military operatives.”

- BBC, 24 November 2026: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c6rrg7ggq190o

## Zaporizhzhia

A Russian overnight strike on Zaporizhzhia on the night of 24 November 2026 killed two people and wounded dozens, including children. Ukrainian officials described damage to apartment buildings and private homes. The wounded count was revised upward through the morning: about 40 in a Ukrainian prosecutor’s 9:10 a.m. update, and 48 in a later AP report.

## South Korea air-defense zone

Several Russian warplanes briefly entered South Korea’s air defense identification zone over the East Sea early Tuesday, approaching Ulleung Island, then left without entering South Korean airspace. The South Korean Air Force scrambled jets. A short local air-raid alert went out and at first misidentified the aircraft.
